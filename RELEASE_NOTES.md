## RichPresence 1.2.0

Now on **Windows, Linux, Android and iPhone**.

### Windows and Linux
- **Keeps your Discord streaks.** For games Discord already knows (like Genshin Impact), RichPresence
  lets Discord detect the game first, so it counts for Recent Activity and streaks, then shows its own
  card (with extras like your Genshin stats) after 2 minutes. You can turn this off in Settings.
- Linux: `RichPresence-x86_64.AppImage` (any distro) or `richpresence_1.2.0_amd64.deb`. Finds Steam,
  Heroic, Lutris and `~/Games` games, and shows music from any player.

### Android (`RichPresence-android.apk`)
- Link your Discord account in the app; it shows the game you're playing and what you're listening to
  (Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music and other players).
- Needs two permissions the app walks you through: Usage access and Notification access.
- Android will warn that the app isn't from the Play Store; that's expected.

### iPhone (`RichPresence-ios-sideload.ipa`)
- Shows your **Apple Music** or **Spotify** song with album art and **live lyrics**, and games through
  Shortcuts automations. Two cards at once (game + music).
- Not on the App Store: install it yourself with [Sideloadly](https://sideloadly.io) and your own Apple ID.
  With a free Apple ID the app has to be reinstalled every 7 days.
- Spotify only works for accounts added to the app in Spotify's developer dashboard (development mode).

### Verify your download
Every file is built by GitHub Actions from this repository's source.
